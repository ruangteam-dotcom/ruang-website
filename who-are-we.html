Isheta Shah
Meet Isheta

Isheta Shah · Clinical Psychologist · MAHPC(CP)00915

Making mental health conversations feel a little less intimidating.

Isheta is a Clinical Psychologist with a Master’s in Clinical Psychology from Taylor’s University and a BSc (Hons) in Psychology and Cognitive Neuroscience from the University of Nottingham.

She believes that people don’t need to wait until things become unbearable before they deserve support.

Her approach

Isheta sees therapy as a space where you can slow down, understand the patterns underneath what you’re experiencing and make sense of the parts of yourself that may not always feel easy to understand.

She takes an integrative approach to therapy, tailoring sessions to each individual’s needs and experiences.

Areas of interest

Anxiety & Mood Difficulties Relationships & Interpersonal Difficulties Self-Esteem & Self-Worth Emotional Regulation Trauma & Complex Experiences Personality-Related Difficulties Life Transitions Autism & Neurodiversity Low Mood & Depression

A little more about Isheta

Outside the therapy room, Isheta is usually somewhere between a workout class, rushing to meet her friends in between work, taking far too many photos and videos of sunsets, or planning the next thing.

She believes mental health conversations don’t have to be heavy all the time. Sometimes they can start with something as simple as:

“How are you, really?”

Puteri Adriana
Meet Puteri

Puteri Adriana · Clinical Psychologist · MAHPC(CP)00916

A clinical psychologist who believes understanding yourself is the first step toward meaningful change.

Puteri is a Clinical Psychologist with a Master’s in Clinical Psychology from Taylor’s University and a BSc (Hons) in Psychology from the University of Manchester.

She believes that mental health care should feel human, accessible and grounded in real life.

Her work focuses on emotional wellbeing, self-esteem, and life transitions, with experience working with children, adolescents and adults navigating anxiety, stress, low mood, interpersonal difficulties and emotional challenges.

Her approach

Therapy isn’t about someone telling you how to live your life.

For Puteri, it’s about creating a space where you can understand yourself better, make sense of what you’re experiencing and explore what might help you move forward.

Areas of interest

Anxiety & Stress Self-esteem & Self-worth Emotional Regulation Relationship Difficulties Life Transitions & Adjustment Low Mood & Depression Social Anxiety Workplace Stress & Burnout Identity & Self-Discovery Autism & Neurodiversity
