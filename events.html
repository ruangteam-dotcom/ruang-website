Events

Where conversations happen.

Mental health doesn’t only belong in a therapy room.

Through ruang events, we create opportunities for people to connect, learn, reflect and have conversations that might otherwise never happen.

From community conversations and mental health screenings to workshops and collaborations, our events are designed to make mental health feel approachable, relevant and part of everyday life.
