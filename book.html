Three steps to your first session.

Pick a time with your psychologist, make your payment, then fill in your registration and consent. It takes about 10 minutes, and we need all three before your session can go ahead.

What happens next

Your calendar invite and session link are in your booking confirmation email. Your psychologist will reach out if anything else is needed.

Changing your appointment

  • Please reschedule or cancel at least 3 working days ahead.
  • Changes with less than 24 hours’ notice are charged the full session fee.
  • If you haven’t joined or contacted us within 15 minutes of the start time, it counts as a no-show.

Need help right now?

ruang is not a 24-hour crisis service. If you are in immediate danger, call 999 or go to the nearest hospital emergency department. You can also reach Talian Kasih on 15999 or Befrienders on 03-7627 2929.
